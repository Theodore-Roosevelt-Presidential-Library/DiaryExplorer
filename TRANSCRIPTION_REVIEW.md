- **0068 · Saturday, February 16, 1884** — entry, medium: Brown ink, fills page; continues on Feb 17 (0069). Last line: the day figure in 'Feb 12th' is retouched/overwritten (second digit heavy and malformed); '12' is the most likely reading. 'to the' is written run together ('tothe'), transcribed as two words. Superscript ordinals inline.
- **0069 · Sunday, February 17, 1884** — entry, medium: Brown ink; continuation of Feb 16 (0068). Line 2: 'on' and '14' are retraced/overwritten in darker, heavier ink (underlying figure not legible). Line 4 begins 'On my mother had' exactly as written. - **0191 · Wednesday, June 18, 1884** — entry, medium: Brown ink, fills the page to the bottom. Line 3: "buck" is written over another word (overwritten letters), hence [?]. "within" (line 15) is smudged/blotted at its start. "across" with long-s form for ss.
- **0348 · Saturday, November 22, 1884** — tally, medium: Numeral heavily overwritten: reads as 6 written over another figure (probably 3, matching the correction); could conceivably be read 8.
- **0351 · Tuesday, November 25, 1884** — entry, medium: '&' is TR's ampersand. Second line reads 'Lang'; the final upward stroke looks like the exit of the 'g' rather than an 's', but 'Langs' cannot be ruled out.
- **0359 · Wednesday, December 3, 1884** — entry, medium: Line 1 has a small mark after 'went' (stray comma?). - **0369 · Saturday, December 13, 1884** — entry, medium: After '-10°' there is a 'Z'-like flourish (possibly a semicolon or end stroke); not transcribed as a letter.
- **0373 · Wednesday, December 17, 1884** — tally, medium: First word of tally abbreviated/cramped, reads 'wht'. Tally counts the prairie chicken as 'grouse'.
- **0390 · Cash Account—January.** — list, medium: Section A, darker brown ink. '&' renders TR's ampersand. Line 10: ditto under 'Merrifield'. Line 12: the two ditto marks are written just below the line (between 12 and B), under 'cow-corral' and 'stock'; assigned to line 12. '(fur coat)': 'fur' probable.
